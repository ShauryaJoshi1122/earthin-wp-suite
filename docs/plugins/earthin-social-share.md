## 1. Overview

Earthin Social Share adds fast, lightweight, and privacy-respecting social sharing buttons to WordPress content without loading third-party tracking scripts or cookies.

---

## 2. Real Features Implemented

* **Supported Sharing Networks**:
  * X (Twitter)
  * Facebook
  * LinkedIn
  * WhatsApp
  * Telegram
  * Reddit
  * Email
* **Flexible Placement**:
  * Below Content (Standard)
  * Above Content
  * Both Above & Below Content
  * Manual placement via `[earthin_social_share]` shortcode.
* **Per-Post-Type Control**:
  * Toggle buttons across Posts, Pages, or custom post types.
* **Zero Tracking Overhead**:
  * Direct official sharing links. Zero JavaScript tracking pixels and zero external network calls.
