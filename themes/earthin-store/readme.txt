High-converting, lightning-fast digital storefront and eCommerce theme for digital products, software downloads, and instant UPI QR payments.

== Description ==
Earthin Store is built specifically for creators, software sellers, and digital publishers. It pairs seamlessly with Earthin Dynamic UPI QR Engine to enable frictionless, zero-fee direct scan payments, digital download fulfillment, and conversion-optimized storefront grids.

== Features ==
* Dynamic store catalog with sale badges & pricing pills
* Built-in instant UPI QR readiness
* Product ratings and customer reviews
* Trust & security guarantees strip
* Zero external bloat, pure vanilla performance

== Installation ==
1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click Add New.
2. Click Upload Theme and choose earthin-store.zip.
3. Click Install Now and Activate.
