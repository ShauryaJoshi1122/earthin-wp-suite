Optimize social media sharing with Open Graph and Twitter Card tags plus a live post preview card in the WordPress editor.

== Description ==

**Earthin Social Preview** ensures your posts and pages look stunning when shared on Facebook, X / Twitter, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and iMessage. Preview exactly how your content will render before hitting publish.

### Key Features

* **Live Editor Card Preview**: Preview social card titles, snippets, and images in real-time within the post editor.
* **Per-Post Overrides**: Specify distinct titles, descriptions, and 1200x630 banner images for social media without changing your on-page text.
* **Open Graph & Twitter Cards**: Clean, standards-compliant meta tags generated automatically in `<head>`.
* **Global Fallback Image**: Never share a blank card; fallback to your branded default social graphic.

== Installation ==

1. Upload `earthin-social-preview` to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate via WordPress Plugins menu.
3. Configure defaults in Settings -> Social Preview.
